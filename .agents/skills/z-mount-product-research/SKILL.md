# Z-mount product research

Deliver evidence-backed decisions and matching canonical records for the requested
products and fields. Apply `AGENTS.md` and its required task-specific rules.
This skill supplies research workflow; schemas and repository policy remain authoritative.

## Read for the task

Locate existing records and research results before choosing a new ID. Consult the
identity and mount registries when resolving names or identifiers. Read the relevant
schema definitions when mapping evidence to fields. For research policy, use the relevant
sections of [inclusion scope](../../../docs/en/overview.md),
[evidence recording](../../../docs/en/research.md), and
[value rules](../../../docs/en/value-rules.md). The paired Japanese pages are primary;
read either edition, not both by default.

- For lenses, teleconverters, pinhole products, and supplied or product-dedicated
  adapter configurations, use [Lens research](references/lenses.md).
- For independently marketed mount-conversion products, use
  [Adapter research](references/adapters.md).

- When inspecting websites or confirming product names, model numbers, or URLs, use
  [Website verification](references/web-review.md).
- For an existing product or research candidate, use
  [Re-research](references/re-research.md). Ordinary re-research starts with
  `needs-review` decisions and `unresolved` questions, then checks existing facts.
- For image-based findings or ambiguous evidence that may need human review, use
  [Image inspection and uncertain findings](references/image-review.md).

Read only the applicable reference and sections. A mixed request uses both routes
for their respective products.

## Find evidence efficiently

For a new product, establish its identity, Nikon Z configuration, and release eligibility
before building its record. Start with exact official product pages and primary documents;
choose fallback sources under the evidence rules linked above.

For existing products, follow the bounded re-research route instead of repeating a new
product investigation. Read additional references only when the finding requires them.

Distinguish firmware-update capability, a USB connection, and an actually published
firmware update. Publication requires an official update file or release entry;
failure to find one does not establish a negative fact.

## Finish the requested research

Within the authorized scope, continue through source review, the research decision,
matching data edits, and validation rather than stopping at the first findings.

Research is complete when the requested products and field groups have been assessed,
material evidence is recorded, canonical facts and source coverage agree, remaining
questions are explicit, and the checks required by `AGENTS.md` pass. Unknown facts may
remain unknown; completeness does not mean filling every field.

Follow relevant available evidence until the requested question is resolved or the
remaining gap has no concrete accepted-source lead. Do not repeat equivalent searches
without a new lead. Record unresolved questions under the repository rules and report
access barriers or schema limitations; do not broaden the assignment to redesign the
schema, publish a release, or research unrelated products.

Review the final diff against the pre-research state. Report established facts and
remaining gaps, distinguishing changes to `null`, removed facts or official page URLs,
and added or removed research notes. Include validation results so gains do not conceal
lost evidence or unresolved work.
