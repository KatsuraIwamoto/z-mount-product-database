# Re-research of existing products and candidates

Use existing canonical records and research results as the starting point. Ordinary
re-research checks recorded information and evaluates new information encountered during
that check; it is not a fresh investigation of every possible field.
An explicit request for a full audit or missing-field investigation expands that scope.
A request limited to a specific correction stays within that scope.

## Priorities

For the requested products, start with `needs-review` inclusion decisions and existing
`unresolved` questions. Check their recorded sources and targeted new official evidence
for the question at issue. Keep the question open if no new accepted-source lead resolves
it; priority does not require repeating an exhausted search indefinitely.

Then check recorded values and links, using existing sources first. Compare numbers,
units, measurement conditions, product names, model numbers, and URL destinations.
Use [Website verification](web-review.md) for the relevant checks. Revisit images when
an existing visual claim or a new finding requires it; do not restart an exterior survey
for every numerical or link correction. When inspecting exterior components, the image
coverage rules still apply.

Evaluate new facts encountered during these checks for the exact product, generation,
mount, and sales configuration. Add them when accepted evidence establishes them and
the current schema can represent them. Expand investigation only as needed to resolve a
specific discrepancy, broken link, identity mismatch, or concrete new lead. Do not search
every `null` field or repeat brand-wide discovery without an explicit broader request.

## Preserve what remains supported

An inaccessible source, a changed layout, or omission from a new photograph does not by
itself invalidate an existing fact. Keep supported values and material sources; report
what could not be rechecked. Conversely, do not preserve a value unquestioningly when
new evidence reveals that its original support is insufficient. Apply the normal value
states and use [human review](image-review.md#escalate-the-uncertain-finding-not-the-whole-task)
when material ambiguity remains. Do not remove an existing question just because the
current pass did not investigate it.

Use `needs-review` when the inclusion decision remains unresolved. If inclusion is
established and only a specification is unknown, retain `included` and record the
field-level question in `unresolved` under the repository rules.

## Review date and completion

Apply the existing `reviewedOn` rules in
[Research results](../../../../docs/en/research.md). Update it only to the actual date
when the current decision and affected sources were rechecked. A changed `checked` list,
a failed retrieval, or a mechanical URL edit alone does not justify advancing the date.
For a targeted pass, recheck the decision and affected evidence before updating the date;
do not imply that every field or source was freshly verified.

In the final report, distinguish the scope checked, resolved and remaining questions,
new facts, corrections or removals, and sources that could not be accessed. Keep workflow
logs and source comparisons out of research notes. Finish when the requested checks and
concrete follow-ups are addressed, uncertainties are recorded, and required validation
passes; filling every unknown field is not a completion condition.

For multi-product work, complete review and updates product by product. If the work ends
before all requested products are reviewed, distinguish completed, partially reviewed,
and not-started products in the final report, with remaining work for partial reviews.
These are report-only progress categories, not replacements for inclusion decisions.
A review may be complete with documented unresolved questions when the requested checks
and validation are finished. Do not advance `reviewedOn` for untouched products or where
the required decision and evidence recheck is incomplete. This reporting convention is
not a reason to stop before completing work that can still proceed.
